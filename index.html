Founder Story

Why I'm Building Pulse

I noticed it almost right away. People weren't choosing skincare based on their own skin. They were buying whatever someone with "good skin" used, with no idea what was going on underneath. And I'm guilty of it too. If I saw something advertised enough, if my favorite influencer mentioned it, or if the packaging was pretty, it ended up on my shelf.

I've spent real money on products that didn't work, and I've gotten bad advice. What I was missing wasn't another product. It was a plan.

So I'm building one. Pulse looks at your skin, builds a routine around what it actually finds, and shows you whether it's working. And nothing ships until it works on every skin tone, including the deeper tones skin tech has gotten wrong the longest.

Building Pulse means the world to me. It's my chance to put something into the world that makes people feel better in their own skin.

Jelyn Andrews, Founder and CEO, Pulse Cosmetic Solutions

FAQ

Questions About Pulse

What is Pulse?

Pulse is an AI skin analysis app. It scans your face, tells you your skin type and what's actually going on, like dark spots, uneven tone, texture or dryness, then gives you a routine plan: which steps, how often, and why, built into an AM and PM routine. Rescan anytime to see whether it's working.

Does Pulse work on darker skin tones?

Yes, and that's the point. Every skin concern Pulse detects is tested across all ten shades of the Monk Skin Tone scale before it reaches the app, with at least 30 consented people per shade, labeled by board-certified dermatologists. If a concern can't meet the bar on every shade, it doesn't ship.

How does the Pulse skin scan work?

A guided face scan walks you through even lighting and positioning, then the photo takes itself. Pulse checks texture, tone, pores and more, and maps where each concern shows up on your face.

Is my face scan data private?

Face scans are biometric data, so Pulse asks for your written consent before any scan and follows Illinois' biometric privacy law everywhere it operates. Scans are private by default, never sold, and deletable anytime. Using your images to train the model is opt-in, and adults only.

Does Pulse sell skincare products?

No. Pulse isn't a store. It tells you what to look for and sends you to the retailers you already shop, and you can keep using products you already own if they fit your routine. Pulse may earn a commission when you buy through a Pulse link.

Who can use Pulse?

Anyone 16 and up who wants a real plan for their skin. It's free to join the waitlist.

What is Pulse for Pros?

Pulse for Pros lets licensed estheticians, medspas and dermatology offices follow the progress of clients who choose to share it, and recommend products and treatments inside Pulse. The founding pilot starts with five licensed estheticians, free.

Is Pulse a medical device?

No. Pulse isn't a medical device and doesn't diagnose skin conditions. For a medical concern, see a dermatologist.

When does Pulse launch?

Pulse is in early access now. Join the waitlist to be first to scan when it opens.
