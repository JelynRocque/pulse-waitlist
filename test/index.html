Before you start

How Pulse uses your face in this test

What this is. A private two-week test of Pulse's skin analysis for people the founder invited. It is not the Pulse app and not a public service.

What we collect. Each day you choose to scan, Pulse takes a photo of your face in your phone's browser, measures face points from it (face geometry) and reads 8 skin measures: dark spots and uneven tone, redness, shine, texture, pores, breakouts, dark circles and radiance. The analysis runs on your phone. The face points never leave your phone. Your scores, and data about the photo's lighting and sharpness, are saved to your test record with your email. Under Illinois and Texas law the face points are biometric identifiers and the scores are biometric information.

Why. Only to test how accurate and consistent Pulse's analysis is across skin tones, and to fix it where it falls short. Never to identify you, never for advertising, never sold.

Who sees it. Pulse (the founder) sees your scores. No outside company receives your photo or face points. Pulse's database provider, Supabase, stores the scores in the United States.

How long. Your scores are kept for 12 months after the test ends, then destroyed. If you withdraw, everything tied to you is destroyed within 30 days. Pulse's full retention rules are at pulseskin.co/biometric-retention.

Your choices. This is optional. You can stop at any time using the Withdraw link on this page or in any email from Pulse, and nothing changes between us.

Access. You have the test page for 14 days from today. Scan every day if you can; even four scans across the two weeks helps.

Questions first? Email hello@pulsecosmeticsolutions.com.
