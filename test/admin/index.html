Add someone who isn't on the waitlist

Adding someone does not send anything. Switch them to Active when you are ready: that creates their private link and emails the invitation (what the test is, that it is not the full app, the consent, the 14 days). Switching to Inactive kills the link; their data stays until they withdraw.
